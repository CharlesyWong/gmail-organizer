A calmer Gmail inbox, one rule at a time.

Gmail Organizer reads message metadata, applies your ordered rules, and adds Auto/<Category> labels. It never downloads message bodies, never deletes mail, and never writes to Gmail unless you pass --apply.

Built around a safety model

Metadata only

From, Subject, Date, labels, and internal date. No bodies, attachments, or contacts.

Dry-run default

See exactly what would change. Gmail writes require an explicit --apply.

Add-only labels

The only mutation is adding a label. Nothing is archived, deleted, or marked read.

Incremental

A local watermark keeps daily runs small, with a one-hour overlap and idempotent checks.

Tested offline

164 tests and 94% coverage against an in-memory Gmail client; CI runs on Ubuntu and Windows.

Local secrets

OAuth tokens, state, and logs stay on your machine and are excluded from git.

What it does not do

It does not read email bodies, send email, archive or delete messages, or upload your mailbox to a server. It is a local command-line tool, not a hosted service.
